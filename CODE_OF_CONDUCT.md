# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in RouteProof a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and learn from them.
- Respect maintainers' time and project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's security reporting channel. Maintainers may remove content or temporarily or permanently exclude contributors whose behavior is inappropriate.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
